Building Your Creator Link Hub

A creator link hub gives people one clear place to find your most important pages, profiles, and contact path.

Beacons is the primary tool used for Trailhead creator link hubs. As a project grows, a creator may also build a links page on their own website.
Put the Most Important Link First
Your main website or store should appear first when you have one.

This gives visitors a direct path to the main place where they can learn about your work, explore your content, or support what you are building.
Organize Your Main Profiles
A clear creator link hub can include your primary social and content profiles in this order:

•  Main website or store.
•  YouTube.
•  TikTok.
•  Instagram or Facebook.
•  Beacons page.
•  Contact path.

Use the platforms that fit your project. You do not need to use every platform to have an organized link hub.
Keep It Clear and Current
Use names that people recognize, keep the links current, and remove anything that no longer represents your work.

A visitor should be able to understand where each link goes before they click it.
Disclose Monetized Links
If a link is connected to an affiliate program, product relationship, or other monetized opportunity, include a short, plain, and visible disclosure.

For example:

“Some links may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.”
A good link hub is simple. It helps people find your main work, follow your content, and know how to contact you without searching through multiple pages.
