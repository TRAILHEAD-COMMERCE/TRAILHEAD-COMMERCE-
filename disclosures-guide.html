Product, Affiliate, and Sponsorship Disclosures

Clear disclosures help people understand when a product, link, or post has a business connection. A short, honest explanation can help build trust with your audience.

Rules can vary by platform and location. When in doubt, say more, not less.
When to Use a Disclosure
Use a clear disclosure when you share a link, product, review, post, or recommendation that may involve a commission, gifted product, paid partnership, sponsorship, or another business relationship.

Keep the wording easy to see and easy to understand.
Affiliate-Link Example
Use this when a link may earn you a commission:

“Some links may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.”
Gifted-Product Example
Use this when you received a product without paying for it:

“Gifted product.”

You may also use:

“This product was provided to me.”
Paid-Partnership Example
Use this when you were paid to create or share content:

“Paid partnership.”

You may also use:

“This content is sponsored.”
Keep It Plain and Visible
A disclosure should be easy for people to notice and understand. Do not hide it in a long paragraph, a distant page, or unclear wording.

Use direct language that matches the relationship involved.
Platform and Location Rules Can Differ
Different platforms and locations may have different disclosure rules. Check the rules that apply to the platform you are using and the place where you are doing business.

Clear disclosure is part of clear communication. Be honest about products, links, and partnerships so people understand the connection.
