How a Trailhead Project Works

Every project begins somewhere: an idea, an early page, a channel, a product, a collection of notes, or a point where you are not sure what comes next.

Trailhead Commerce helps you organize the pieces around what you are building. You choose the support you need. Your project remains yours at every step.
Step 1: Tell Us What You Are Building
Start with the basics. Tell us about your idea, your project name, what you already have, where you feel stuck, and what you would like to move forward.

You do not need a finished plan before starting a conversation.
Step 2: Choose the Help You Need
You choose the kind of support that fits your project. That may involve content organization, page planning, link organization, practical project preparation, or technical coordination.

Trailhead Commerce does not take over your project automatically. Support is selected around the work you want help with.
Step 3: Keep Your Ownership
Your name, content, accounts, audience, and direction remain yours.

Trailhead Commerce coordinates work around your project, but ownership does not silently change hands. Any different arrangement would need to be clearly stated in a specific written agreement.
Step 4: Coordinate the System
Trailhead helps organize the moving pieces behind a project, including content organization, page plans, links, and technical work through Basecamp Embed.

Basecamp Embed is a Trailhead Commerce service for placing approved content into webpages and helping keep the technical side of a project moving.
Step 5: Build With Approved Content
Your approved content becomes the project. This may include your writing, images, videos, links, product information, pages, and other materials you choose to provide.

Trailhead uses client material only with approval.
Step 6: Review Before Going Live
You review and approve the project before it goes live. After launch, you choose only the ongoing support you want.

You remain involved in the decisions that affect your work.
What Stays Confidential
Every project is different. To protect clients, partners, and internal business systems, Trailhead Commerce keeps its methods, contacts, sourcing details, negotiations, research, and internal systems confidential.
A clear project does not require having every answer from day one. Start with what you have, identify what you need, and take the next useful step.
