How It Works

You do not have to figure out every tool, page, account, link, form, and workflow by yourself. Trailhead Commerce helps independent creators, brands, channels, reviewers, and online projects organize the business side of their work. You stay in control of your identity, content, and direction.
“Bring the idea. We help build the path.”
Step 1 — Tell Us What You Are Building
You may have a channel, a website, a product idea, a review project, or content that needs a better home. Start by telling us what you have, what you want to build, and where you feel stuck.
Step 2 — Choose the Help You Need
Some projects only need a simple website plan. Others need content organization, link hubs, outreach, or a bigger project system. You choose the support you want — Trailhead never takes over automatically.
Step 3 — Keep Your Ownership
Your name, content, website, accounts, audience, and original work stay yours unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. We help coordinate the work — we do not automatically own your project.
Step 4 — We Help Coordinate the System
With your permission, we help organize content, build page plans, coordinate links, and manage project details. For website embedding and technical support, Trailhead handles the work through its Basecamp Embed service — keeping communication, updates, and requests organized.
Step 5 — Your Content Becomes Part of the Project
You provide or approve the photos, videos, writing, reviews, product information, and links used for your project. Trailhead organizes that approved material into pages, tools, and online systems that match your goals.
Step 6 — Review, Launch, and Keep Moving
Nothing goes live without your review and approval. After launch, you choose only the ongoing support you want — updates, new pages, content organization, technical coordination, or campaign help.
