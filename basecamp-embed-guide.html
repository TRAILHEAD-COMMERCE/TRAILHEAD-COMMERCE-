Working With Basecamp Embed

Basecamp Embed is a Trailhead Commerce service for placing approved content into webpages and helping keep the technical side of a project moving.

Embedding means placing things like videos, feeds, or forms directly into your webpage so visitors can use them without going somewhere else.
What Basecamp Embed Can Help Coordinate
Basecamp Embed helps coordinate the placement of approved content into a webpage. Depending on the project, this may include embedded videos, feeds, forms, links, and other page content.

The work is coordinated around the content, page plan, and updates that have been approved for the project.
Embedding and Tracking Are Different
Embedding places content into a page.

Traffic and sales analytics measure what visitors do, such as visits, clicks, or sales.

Basecamp Embed handles the placement of content. Traffic and sales analytics are a separate option, included only where a project calls for it and always set by written agreement.
Your Options
Embedding is a general type of technical work that can also be handled by outside contractors. When the work runs through Trailhead Commerce, it is provided under the Basecamp Embed name.

The right option depends on the project, the tools involved, and the support you choose.
Approved Content Comes First
Your approved content guides the technical work. You review and approve the materials and changes connected to your project before they go live.
Basecamp Embed helps keep content placement organized, practical, and connected to the project you are building.
