New Project Checklist

Every good project starts with a clear foundation. Before reaching out, take a few minutes to organize the basics of what you are building, who it is for, and where you would like it to go.

You do not need every answer yet. A clear idea, an honest goal, and a willingness to build are enough to begin.
Project Basics
•  What is the name of the project, brand, page, product, or idea?
•  What are you trying to create, improve, launch, or grow?
•  Who is the project for?
•  What problem, interest, need, or opportunity does it serve?
•  What makes the project different from similar ideas?
Where You Are Now
•  What stage are you in right now?
•  Do you already have content, products, a website, social pages, or an audience?
•  What has already been completed?
•  What are you still trying to figure out?
Your Next Direction
•  What would you like help organizing first?
•  What would progress look like over the next few months?
•  What is the most useful next step for the project?
