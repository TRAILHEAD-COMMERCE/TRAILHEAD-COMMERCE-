Getting Your Content Ready

Before a page, site, link hub, or project can be organized, it helps to gather the material that explains who you are and what you do.

Bring what you have. It does not need to be polished. A clear project name, a short description, and your links are a strong place to start.
Start With the Basics
Have these items ready if possible:

•  A clear project, page, brand, channel, or business name.
•  A short description of who you are and what you do.
•  Your social-media links and usernames.
•  Your current website or store link, if you have one.
Gather Your Visual Material
Collect the images and visual examples that best represent your work:

•  Photos.
•  Videos.
•  Logo files.
•  Graphics.
•  Product images.
•  Any existing colors, fonts, or visual style you use.
Show Your Work
Examples help show what the project is about. Gather any useful materials, including:

•  Product details.
•  Reviews.
•  Examples of your best work.
•  Posts, articles, videos, or pages you are proud of.
•  Links to current projects, channels, stores, or social profiles.
Bring Your Direction
Notes and early ideas are useful. Bring any goals, plans, questions, or thoughts you have collected.

You do not need a complete strategy. Knowing what you want to build, improve, share, or organize is enough to begin.
Content does not have to be perfect before it becomes useful. Gather the basics, bring the examples you have, and let the next steps become clearer from there.
